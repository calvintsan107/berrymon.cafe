Three Cultivar Blend
The Art of Matcha Blending
Okumidori · Shizuoka
Fresh & vivid color, gentle sweetness balanced with a touch of astringency. Only 3% of Japan's tea-growing area.
Asahi · Kyoto
Large, thin and soft leaves with a silky touch. Young buds produce rich aroma and deep flavor — rare and difficult to cultivate.
Saemidori · Kagoshima
Low astringency, full-bodied and easy drinking, with a strong character and nut-like aroma. Born in warm climates, beloved for its extraordinary flavor.
Our Philosophy

Where Tea Meets Craft

"Each matcha variety is a conversation between soil, climate, and the hands that tend it."

At Berrymoon, we source three distinct Japanese tea cultivars and blend them with precision to create our signature matcha profiles. Whether you prefer the bold character of Nguyệt, the balanced elegance of Phong, or the sweet richness of Hoa and Sasae — each cup tells a different story of Japanese tea terroir.

Our premium matcha is whisked fresh to order using traditional chasen bamboo whisks, unlocking the full umami depth and vibrantly green color that only ceremonial-grade matcha can deliver.
